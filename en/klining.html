How it works

Collect a checklist in 10 minutes

The cloud dashboard opens in the browser, with nothing to deploy on your servers. Build sections and questions visually: yes/no, number, date, choice, scale or file. Mark required items and questions that need photo evidence.

Assign and schedule

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to launch?

The first checklist takes about 10 minutes. A full launch takes one working day: we create the organization, locations and employees, then import your checklists. You can add the rest yourself without a separate implementation project.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation is required for administrators: the cloud service opens in any browser. There are no servers to deploy, workstation licences to buy or implementation consultants to hire. Employees can use the mobile web app (PWA) from a link, add it to the home screen and receive updates automatically. Native iOS and Android apps are also available.

Do employees need to be trained?

No. The contractor sees a list of assigned checks in the application and fills out the form; these are two screens. Only the administrator needs training and it takes about an hour

What if there is no connection at the site?

The app stores replies and attachments locally and sends them to the server when the network is available. The verification can be completed completely offline

Is it possible to upload photos from the gallery?

No. If this mode is enabled, the camera opens directly from the checklist, and time and coordinates are attached to the photo. This is what distinguishes a real check from a formal one.

My checklists are complex, they are not just “yes/no”. Will it fit?

Yes. This is a core use case. Number ranges, decimal precision, dates, scales, multiple choice and files are configured for each question, including required answers and validation rules.

Where is the data stored?

On servers in Russia

Some of our employees do not read Russian well. What to do?

The app interface follows the employee’s language selected in their profile. Checklists can be maintained in any language: employees see the version they understand, while you receive the results in your dashboard.

Why is it cheaper than competitors?

We spent years building service processes for major events, catering and IT operations. The product grew from tools we used ourselves, so there is no greenfield development or oversized sales team built into the price. Your starting price remains yours.

Are there any integrations?

There is an open API for uploading test results. We discuss ready-made connectors as part of the corporate tariff
